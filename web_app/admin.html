Connect a 100% free Google Firebase Realtime Database URL to update leaves directly from your phone. Any professor you mark on leave will instantly reflect for every SRCC student live without downloading or re-deploying anything! Cost is ₹0 forever (free tier allows millions of requests).

ℹ️ 1-Minute Guide: How to get your free Cloud URL (₹0 cost, No card required)
  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com using your Google account.
  2. Click "Add project", enter project name (e.g. srcc-timetable), and proceed (Google Analytics optional).
  3. In the left sidebar, navigate to Build → Realtime Database, then click Create Database (choose default location).
  4. In security rules step, select "Start in test mode" (gives open read/write access) and click Enable.
  5. Copy your database URL at the top (looks like https://srcc-timetable-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com/), append leaves.json at the end, and paste it above!
  6. Click "Save & Test Connection". Done! Now whenever you mark a leave from your phone, all SRCC students see it immediately.
